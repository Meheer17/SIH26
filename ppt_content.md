### Slide 2: The Core Challenge in Indian Healthcare
- **Slide Title**: The 4 Critical Healthcare Gaps in India
- **Visual**: 4 warning cards highlighting real-world statistics.
- **Key Points**:
  - **Rural Diagnostic Gap**: 70% of rural India lacks access to rapid laboratory diagnostic facilities for anemia & respiratory screening.
  - **OPD Time Crisis**: Doctors get average 2 minutes per patient in Indian government OPDs; clinical history capture is incomplete.
  - **Silent Personnel Burnout**: High operational stress and suicides in CAPF/Armed Forces go undetected due to manual reporting.
  - **Atrocity Victim Trauma**: SC/ST atrocity victims face legal delays and evidence tampering during long judicial proceedings.
